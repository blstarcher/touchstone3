Welcome to North Star Bakery

Fresh Baking for Our Community

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery serving handmade breads, pastries, and cakes. We bake in small batches throughout the day so our customers can enjoy fresh products made with care.

Whether you are stopping in on your morning commute, choosing a weekend treat for your family, or planning food for an event, we are happy to help.

What We Are Known For

  • Fresh artisan breads baked in small batches
  • Handmade pastries prepared each morning
  • Custom cakes for birthdays and community events
  • Friendly service and locally sourced ingredients

Featured Item of the Week

Honey Oat Signature Loaf

This week's featured item is our Honey Oat Signature Loaf. It combines a lightly sweet flavor with a soft center and a golden crust. It is a good choice for toast, sandwiches, or sharing at the dinner table.

Quantities are limited, so customers may submit a pre-order request before visiting.
