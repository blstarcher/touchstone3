About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small neighborhood business built around a love of handmade bread and a desire to serve the local community. What started with a few family recipes has grown into a bakery offering breads, pastries, and custom cakes for customers throughout the area.

We want each visit to feel welcoming and personal. Our team enjoys learning the names of regular customers, helping families select treats, and preparing orders for important celebrations.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good baked products begin with good ingredients. Whenever practical, we work with local and regional suppliers for flour, eggs, dairy products, fruit, honey, and other ingredients.

Baking in small batches helps us maintain quality, reduce waste, and provide fresher products throughout the day. Seasonal ingredients also allow us to offer changing flavors that reflect the time of year.

  • Small-batch preparation
  • Local and regional sourcing when available
  • Seasonal ingredients
  • Careful attention to freshness and quality

Meet Our Staff

Emma Carter, Head Baker

Emma has more than 15 years of baking experience and leads the bakery's daily bread production. She is especially interested in naturally fermented dough and traditional shaping methods.

Liam Brooks, Pastry Chef

Liam prepares the bakery's croissants, muffins, cinnamon rolls, and seasonal pastries. He enjoys developing new products using locally available fruit.

Maya Reed, Cake Decorator

Maya works directly with customers to create cakes for birthdays, weddings, showers, and community events. She helps customers select designs that fit their occasion and budget.
